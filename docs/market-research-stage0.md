## 1. Market Size — TAM / SAM / SOM

### TAM — Field Sales Software (Global)

The global **field sales software** market is the cleanest proxy for D2D Blitz's addressable universe.

| Source | 2024/25 Estimate | 2030–2033 Projection | CAGR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Virtue Market Research | $1.79 B (2025) | $8.2 B by 2033 | 10.3% |
| Verified Market Research | $2.5–3.5 B (2024) | $9.6 B by 2030 | 12.5% |

**Chosen TAM anchor:** ~$2.5 B globally (2025), growing at ~11% CAGR. The broader sales enablement market sits at $5–6 B; field/D2D is a meaningful but minority slice.

The global **direct sales industry** (all channels) was ~$208 B in 2025 (6.6% CAGR projected through 2029) — this is the economic activity D2D software sits on top of.

### SAM — D2D-Specific Operations Software (US-focused)

Door-to-door sales is a distinct motion from general field sales. The US accounts for a disproportionate share of commission-heavy, blitz-style D2D operations (solar, security, pest, telecom). Applying a 15% segment weight to the global field sales market and a ~45% US share:

> **SAM ≈ $170 M** (US D2D operations software, 2025)

Validation proxy: SalesRabbit — the category's revenue leader — reported $5 M ARR in 2024 with 85,000+ active users. If the market leader captures ~3% of SAM, a $170 M SAM is plausible. It also signals the category is early and fragmented.

### SOM — Realistic 3-Year Target for D2D Blitz

D2D Blitz differentiates on **blitz campaign management + commission/payout automation**, a combination no current competitor offers. Targeting mid-market organizations (20–500 reps):

- Estimated reachable orgs with 20+ reps: ~3,000–5,000 in the US
- Conversion target (3 years): 150–300 orgs
- Blended ACV: ~$12,000–$24,000/year (estimated $50–100/seat/mo × 20–40 avg seats)

> **SOM ≈ $2 M–$7 M ARR** at 3 years — comparable to SalesRabbit's trajectory from 2021–2024.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary Buyer Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | VP of Sales / Director of Field Operations / Market Owner |
| **Org size** | 20–300 field reps, 2–10 markets |
| **Business model** | Commission-only or performance-pay D2D reps; installs/activations drive revenue |
| **Sales motion** | Multi-week "blitz" campaigns in a territory; reps may travel and require housing |
| **Current tools** | Spreadsheets (commissions), SalesRabbit/Spotio (canvassing), QuickBooks (payroll) — all siloed |

### Top Verticals

**1. Solar Sales Organizations** _(highest priority)_  
D2D is the primary customer acquisition channel for residential solar. The industry is growing rapidly (IRA incentives, rising electricity costs) and staffing up aggressively. "Sales only" companies that sign contracts and hand off to EPCs are pure D2D operations. Hundreds of regional installers + dozens of national pure-play D2D solar outfits.

**2. Pest Control** _(large, established)_  
34,076 pest control businesses in the US (IBISWorld, 2026); $24.5 B market. Summer D2D campaigns ("blitz season") are standard practice at regional/national operators (Anticimex, Hawx, Clark, etc.). Commission structures are complex and manual.

**3. Home Security / Smart Home** _(high-value, brand-driven)_  
Companies like Vivint, Guardian, Brinks, and hundreds of authorized dealers deploy D2D armies. Dealer agreements create multi-tier commission structures — exactly what D2D Blitz's "stack" model handles.

**4. Broadband / ISP / Telecom** _(growing post-ACP)_  
Starlink resellers, fiber overbuilders (Metronet, Brightspeed), and cable subcontractors all use D2D. Per-install economics and carrier reconciliation are painful.

**5. Energy / Deregulated Markets** _(niche but high-value)_  
Electricity and gas choice states (TX, PA, IL, OH) support large D2D energy broker operations with complex commission splits.

### Core Pain Points

1. **Commission opacity and errors:** Reps distrust spreadsheet-calculated pay. Manual errors and delayed payouts cause top-rep churn. No current tool handles carrier reconciliation → commission payout end-to-end.
2. **Blitz logistics fragmentation:** Org-wide campaigns spanning travel, housing, rep assignments, and expense management are tracked in 3–5 separate systems (or group texts).
3. **Rep turnover (35% industry average, ~3× other industries):** Without recruiting pipelines and governance tracking, organizations constantly restart from scratch.
4. **Install reconciliation lag:** Carriers upload install files days or weeks after the sale; matching installs to reps for commission calculation is error-prone and slow.
5. **Visibility gaps for leadership:** No single dashboard shows blitz profitability, governance tiers, recruiter ROI, and payout status together.

## 3. Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Type | Pricing | Core Strength | Key Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SalesRabbit** | D2D CRM / canvassing | $49/user/mo (annual); $59/mo billed monthly; Enterprise custom | Market leader, 85k+ users, territory mapping, GPS tracking, DataGrid AI | No commission management, no blitz lifecycle, no install reconciliation |
| **SPOTIO** | Field sales CRM | ~$39/user/mo (Team) to $69/user/mo (Business); Enterprise custom | Strong lead management, route optimization, CRM integrations | No commission/payout tooling, limited D2D-specific blitz features |
| **Knockbase** | D2D canvassing | Not public (demo required) | All-in-one canvassing, dynamic routing, multi-vertical | Newer entrant, limited commission/payout depth |
| **Canvass** (canvass.com) | Solar D2D CRM | Not public | Solar-native (title check, EPC integrations, financing), mobile-first | Vertical-locked to solar; no blitz staffing, no commission engine |
| **Blitz** (blitzrocks.com) | Commission automation | Not public (SaaS, SMB-focused) | Commission calculation automation, comp plan flexibility | Not D2D-specific; no territory management, no blitz ops, no field app |
| **Ecanvasser** | Canvassing platform | Not public | 200,000+ organizers in 70 countries; strong routing and data tools | Political-canvassing DNA; weak on commission/pay, not D2D-sales-native |

### Competitive Gap D2D Blitz Fills

No competitor combines:
- Blitz campaign lifecycle (Planning → Staffing → Active → Closed)
- Commission stack calculation with carrier install reconciliation
- Governance tier management (install-rate thresholds → comp multipliers)
- Rep recruiting pipeline
- Payout batch workflow with deduction management

SalesRabbit's $5 M ARR ceiling despite 85k users suggests the market chronically underpays for canvassing-only tools. The commission/payout layer — where real financial risk lives — is a premium wedge that incumbents have not addressed.

## 5. Key Insights

- **The category leader (SalesRabbit) caps at $5 M ARR with 85,000 users.** This signals chronic undermonetization — D2D orgs pay for canvassing tools but not yet for operations/commission infrastructure. D2D Blitz targets the higher-value, higher-willingness-to-pay layer.

- **Commission management is the highest-pain, least-served workflow.** Every vertical cites pay opacity and calculation errors as top-2 rep retention killers. No current D2D tool touches payroll/payout end-to-end; D2D Blitz's stack model + payout batch workflow is a genuine wedge.

- **Solar is the fastest-growing and most software-hungry vertical.** Explosive hiring, complex EPC/financing integrations, regulatory pressure (multiple AG actions for D2D misconduct), and high transaction values make solar D2D orgs the ideal beachhead ICP.

- **Rep turnover (35%, 3× the industry average) creates compounding demand.** High churn means D2D orgs constantly recruit, onboard, and train — D2D Blitz's recruiting pipeline + governance tiers directly address this cycle, making the platform stickier than a point canvassing tool.

- **The field sales software market is growing at 10–12% CAGR and still fragmented.** No competitor has achieved escape velocity (SalesRabbit at $5 M is the leader). The window for a differentiated operations platform — not just a mapping/CRM tool — is open.
